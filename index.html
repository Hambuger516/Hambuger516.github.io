Making AI systems work.

I am a PhD researcher in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at The University of Manchester, supervised by Zhongguo Li and Zhengtao Ding. My research interests are embodied AI, distributed systems, large language model inference, and optimization.

I develop scheduling and control methods for efficient AI and distributed computing, with particular attention to latency, memory capacity, and heterogeneous resources. Before my PhD, I received an M.E. in Control Science and Engineering from Beihang University and a B.E. in Automation from Jilin University.

02Selected research experience

Jul 2025 – Present

LLM serving control and scheduling

PhD research · The University of Manchester

  • Study admission control, KV-cache capacity, and heterogeneous GPU scheduling under first-token and per-token latency constraints.
  • Develop control and optimization methods for pipeline placement, routing, and efficient resource use in distributed LLM serving.

Jun 2024 – Jun 2025

Distributed simulation and task-dependent load balancing

Graduate research · Beihang University

  • Built graph-based and control-oriented models for task allocation, resource balance, and distributed simulation scheduling.
  • Developed CGWSA scheduling strategies and evaluated them using AnyLogic and distributed simulation experiments.

Jan 2023 – Jun 2024

Distributed messaging and data-sharing systems

Research projects · Beihang University

  • Designed Kafka/Redis-based data-sharing and concurrent-access components for distributed simulation systems.
  • Developed DMGA and DMGA-PSO for Kafka configuration optimization, with automated deployment and performance testing.
